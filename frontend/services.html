FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our custom website design and web engineering services.

What types of websites do you engineer?

We build corporate showcase websites, NGO platforms, school websites, automated booking platforms, e-commerce storefronts, and bespoke full-stack applications with secure databases.

Can you integrate South African payment options?

Yes, we integrate leading South African payment providers including PayFast, Yoco, and PayGate to enable secure debit/credit card and instant EFT transactions.

Do you provide website maintenance and hosting?

Yes. We offer once-off website updates at R450 per hour, monthly website support at R750, and hosting from R180 per month. We assess your website and agree on the scope before starting. Technical maintenance, backups and security services are confirmed separately in your quote.

Do I need to move my hosting to Zazele?

No. You can use our website update services while keeping your existing hosting, provided we have the access needed to complete the work.
