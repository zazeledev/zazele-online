Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding enrollment, accreditation, and the learning portal.

Are Zazele computer courses accredited in South Africa?

Yes, our courses are accredited by the Media, Information and Communication Technologies Sector Education and Training Authority (MICT SETA) of South Africa.

What are the entry requirements?

There are no strict entry requirements. Our curriculum is designed to take students from basic computer operations through to professional workplace productivity.

How does the Pay-As-You-Learn option work?

You can start with an upfront registration fee of R1,000, and complete installment payments as you progress through the 7 modules at your own pace.
