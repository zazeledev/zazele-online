About Zazele

Technology is useful
when people can use it.

We build websites and teach digital skills. Both start with the same question: what will help someone take their next step?

Rooted in learning.
Focused on building.

Zazele began in digital education in 2011. Today, our work brings together practical computer training and website development for businesses and organisations in South Africa.

Our studio designs and develops websites and custom applications. Our academy helps learners build confidence with the digital tools used at work and in everyday life.
